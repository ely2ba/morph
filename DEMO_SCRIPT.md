# Morph — final video script

**Target runtime:** approximately 2 minutes 20 seconds

**Deliverable:** one public YouTube video with narration

**Recording size:** 1440 × 900, browser zoom 100%

Hearth & Home is the one complete, uninterrupted WebMCP demonstration. Wayline and The Current are short proofs that the same interaction model works on completely different websites. Record the routes as separate clean takes and edit them into one video.

Keep the real ChatGPT browser assistant visible when the two Hearth & Home requests are sent. Turn off **System Settings → Accessibility → Display → Reduce motion**, close unrelated tabs and notifications, and begin Hearth & Home from its clean-start URL. Keep Send, roughly two seconds of honest assistant activity, and the full site-native transformation; remove only dead latency before the tool call.

Before recording the two range proofs, prepare their composed states with the prompts at the end of this document. Record an ordinary clean-start shot and a separate completed shot for each. Do not wait for either assistant call on camera.

## 0:00–0:14 — Show Morph working immediately

**Open:** https://morph.carry-protocol.workers.dev/?fresh=1

**Picture and action**

- Begin on the ordinary Hearth & Home catalog with **28 results** and several product cards visible.
- Hold for no more than two seconds.
- Open the real ChatGPT browser assistant with the request already copied, paste it, and press Send.
- Keep both the page and assistant visible through the actual WebMCP call and the beginning of the transformation.

**Exact assistant request**

> I need a washer for a 60 × 62 × 85 cm alcove. I do five loads a week, pay 29p/kWh and 0.4p per litre for water, keep appliances for eight years, and need delivery within four days. Recompose this catalog into a decision page with editable assumptions, a price-versus-noise chart, a shortlist ranked by total ownership cost, and exact exclusions.

**Narration**

> “This store gives me twenty-eight washing machines and expects me to turn specifications, dimensions, delivery dates, and running costs into a decision. Instead, I describe the decision once.”

**On-screen caption:** `One request → a different interface`

## 0:14–0:29 — Reveal the composed decision page

**Picture and action**

- Let the page’s reading, calculating, and composing states remain visible.
- Do not scroll during the site-native transition.
- Keep the assistant’s real `create_decision_view` activity visible for a beat.
- When the page settles, frame the new heading and **9 of 28 machines fit**.

**Narration**

> “Hearth & Home exposes its records, calculations, actions, and native components through WebMCP. The assistant composes them around my goal, while the store still controls every fact. Twenty-eight products become nine real matches.”

**On-screen caption:** `28 products → 9 that fit`

## 0:29–0:44 — Show facts the old interface never calculated

**Picture and action**

- Scroll smoothly past the price-versus-noise chart to the first-ranked **Elmridge E8 Eco**.
- Click **Estimated ownership cost £1,081**.
- Hold on the complete **£1,081.40** calculation, with the purchase, electricity, water, and final addition visible.

**Narration**

> “The page has created the comparison I needed, including facts its original catalog never showed. The E8’s eight-year cost is one thousand eighty-one pounds forty, and every step is inspectable retailer arithmetic.”

**On-screen caption:** `£499 today → £1,081.40 over eight years`

## 0:44–0:57 — Give the person authority

**Picture and action**

- Close the calculation.
- Click **Shortlist** on the Elmridge E8 Eco.
- Return to the assumptions and click the lock beside **Installed depth: 62 cm**.
- Pause briefly after each visible confirmation.

**Narration**

> “I shortlist the E8 and lock the depth I measured. Those are my decisions. A later request can reorganize the page, but it cannot silently erase or overwrite them.”

**On-screen caption:** `Human choices stay protected`

## 0:57–1:13 — Ask again and visibly recompose

**Picture and action**

- Stay on the same page. Do not reload.
- Open the browser assistant, paste the follow-up, and press Send.
- Keep the page visible while the chart and ranked grid give way to a simpler recommendation, calculation, tradeoffs, and delivery view.

**Exact follow-up request**

> Recompose this as a simple brief for my parents. Put a compact summary of my saved and locked choices first, then one recommendation, the saved washer’s exact eight-year cost, plain-language tradeoffs, and a delivery calendar. Preserve my saved washer and locked depth.

**Narration**

> “Now I ask for a much simpler interface for my parents. The same webpage restructures itself again around a different need, without starting the decision over.”

## 1:13–1:28 — Prove continuity

**Picture and action**

- Frame the top of the new composition.
- Point once to **1 saved by you** and once to the lock beside **Installed depth: 62 cm**.
- Scroll just enough to show the E8 recommendation and exact £1,081.40 breakdown together.

**Narration**

> “My shortlisted washer and locked measurement survive. This is the collaboration loop: ask, compose, edit, then ask again. The assistant owns composition, the website owns truth, and the person keeps authority.”

**On-screen caption:** `Assistant = composition · Website = truth · Human = authority`

## 1:28–1:41 — Prove the model works for travel

**Picture and action**

- Cut from one second of the ordinary Wayline results to its already-composed complete-journey view.
- Frame **1h 10m in search → 4h 26m door to door** and briefly open **See every minute**.
- Do not show an assistant request or wait on this route.

**Narration**

> “The same model works for travel. A one-hour-ten advertised flight becomes the four-hour-twenty-six journey a person actually takes, including every walk, wait, transfer, buffer, and luggage step.”

**On-screen caption:** `1h 10m advertised → 4h 26m door to door`

## 1:41–1:54 — Prove the model works for information

**Picture and action**

- Cut from one second of The Current’s ordinary 30-article homepage to its already-composed finite edition.
- Hold on **5 developments · 9m 48s** and one original-reporting or provenance marker.
- Do not show an assistant request or wait on this route.

**Narration**

> “And on a news site, thirty overlapping articles become five distinct developments in nine minutes forty-eight, while original reporting, disagreements, and the source of every verified fact stay visible.”

**On-screen caption:** `30 articles → 5 developments · 9m 48s`

## 1:54–2:20 — State the larger idea

**Open:** https://morph.carry-protocol.workers.dev/showcase

**Picture and action**

- Show all three transformations together in the showcase hero.
- Cut to the collaboration-loop section during the second sentence.
- Briefly overlay: `Recipes`, `Public services`, `Retail`, `Learning`, `Any cooperating website`.
- End on the Morph title and tagline, then hold silently for one second.

**Narration**

> “These three sites prove a larger idea. Any cooperating website can expose trusted facts, actions, and native components, then let each person ask for the interface their task requires. A recipe can become a timed plan. A government site can reveal only the rules that apply. Interfaces no longer have to predict every person and task in advance. Morph lets the page become the interface you need.”

**Final on-screen caption:**

`Morph`

`The page becomes the interface you need.`

## Editing rules

- Submit one final video. The route recordings are only source takes.
- Preserve the full Hearth & Home request-to-transformation and follow-up-to-recomposition sequences.
- Use direct cuts between stable views. Do not add flashy stock transitions.
- Wayline and The Current should take roughly thirteen seconds each, including their ordinary-to-composed cut.
- Do not show developer tools, schemas, JSON, localhost, this script, or unrelated browser tabs.
- Burn in only the short captions specified above.
- Keep the assistant narrow enough that the webpage remains legible.
- Speak calmly at roughly 145–150 words per minute. Do not rush the three transformations or the trust line.
